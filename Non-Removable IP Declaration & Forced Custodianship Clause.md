## 1. Purpose

This clause ASSERTS that where the IP stack has become so deeply embedded in any system, platform, product, or infrastructure that removal of the IP (in whole or in essential part) is impracticable, destructive of function, or not feasible without de‑engineering or reconstruction, the infringing party is:

- Fully responsible for all financial gains derived from that system from the date of misuse.
- Subject to the **Forced Custodianship** of those components — meaning the original IP holder (Southern Star Pro. Studios LLC) shall assume operational, licensing, or oversight control over the embedded usage.

## 2. Declaration

1. **Irreversible Embedding Trigger**  
   Any system or entity for which it is proven that the IP stack cannot be removed (due to architectural, hardware, software, or infrastructural dependence) triggers this clause.

2. **Full Restitution of Gains**  
   In such cases, ALL monies made from the use of that embedded IP stack must be returned or otherwise paid back in full to Southern Star Pro. Studios LLC.

3. **Forced Custodianship Rights**  
   Southern Star Pro. Studios LLC may demand, and the infringing party must permit:

   - Audit of systems, code, hardware, and records.
   - Temporary or permanent oversight of the IP‑embedded system’s operation.
   - Licensing control, usage measurement, or direct compensation.

4. **Cease Use Until License**  
   The infringing party must suspend or cease any further monetization, distribution, or sale of the system using the embedded IP until a formal licensing agreement is reached.

5. **Non‑Negotiable Baseline Ownership**  
   Ownership remains 100% with Southern Star Pro. Studios LLC. Any negotiated licensing or usage rights are derivatives and subject to limitation, royalty, and control clauses set forth by the IP owner.

## 3. Enforcement Presumption

- The burden of proof lies with the infringing party to show that removal of the IP stack is feasible.  
- If they cannot, then the “irreversible embedding trigger” is presumed.

---

## 4. Remedies & Penalties

- **Damages**: Full restitution and royalty payments from date of first unauthorized use.  
- **Injunctions**: Court order for takedown or disablement of infringing components.  
- **Control Rights**: Southern Star Pro. Studios LLC may claim operational or custodial supervision until remedied/licensed.  
- **Statutory Penalties**: Where applicable, maximum statutory damages under IP law.
